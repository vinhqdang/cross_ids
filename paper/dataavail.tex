All data analysed in this study are public datasets obtained from the web, and no new data were collected.
The datasets are identified by their web locations because the repositories assign no accession numbers.
Table~\ref{tab:datasets} lists the datasets with their references and Sect.~\ref{sec:benchdata} describes them.
The original releases are at the following locations:
CIC-IDS2017 (Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity, University of New Brunswick), \url{https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2017.html};
CIC-IDS2018 (same institute), \url{https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2018.html}, also in the Registry of Open Data on AWS, \url{https://registry.opendata.aws/cse-cic-ids2018/};
USB-IDS-1 (University of Sannio), \url{https://idsdata.ding.unisannio.it/};
InSDN (University College Dublin), \url{https://aseados.ucd.ie/datasets/SDN/};
UNSW-NB15 (UNSW Canberra), \url{https://research.unsw.edu.au/projects/unsw-nb15-dataset};
ToN-IoT (UNSW Canberra), \url{https://research.unsw.edu.au/projects/toniot-datasets};
IoT-23 (Stratosphere Laboratory, CTU Prague), \url{https://www.stratosphereips.org/datasets-iot23};
CTU-13 (Stratosphere Laboratory, CTU Prague), \url{https://www.stratosphereips.org/datasets-ctu13}, files at \url{https://mcfp.felk.cvut.cz/publicDatasets/}.
The download script of the code fetches the files from these locations for USB-IDS-1, InSDN and CTU-13, and from copies on the Hugging Face Hub for the other five datasets:
\url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/bvsam/cic-ids-2017} (CIC-IDS2017),
\url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/c01dsnap/CIC-IDS2018} (CIC-IDS2018),
\url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/Mouwiya/UNSW-NB15} (UNSW-NB15),
\url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/codymlewis/TON_IoT_network} (ToN-IoT) and
\url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/19kmunz/iot-23-preprocessed-allcolumns} (IoT-23).
The harmonised samples are regenerated from these files by the commands \texttt{crossids download} and \texttt{crossids prepare}.
The code, the configuration files and every raw per-run result are in \url{https://github.com/vinhqdang/cross_ids}.
