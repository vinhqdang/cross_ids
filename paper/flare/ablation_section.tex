\section{Ablations}\label{sec:ablation}
We retrain FLARE-2 with one component changed and evaluate on the same support draws as the main results (checked: the RF baseline reproduces the earlier numbers exactly).
This costs one training run per cell, so we use a single meta-training seed and two targets (CTU-13 and CIC-IDS2017).
The study therefore shows direction, not significance.

Table~\ref{tab:ablation} shows four things.
\emph{Slot permutation.} Removing the random class-to-slot assignment matches FLARE-2 on CTU-13 (differences of at most 0.016 AUC) but is unstable on CIC-IDS2017: binary AUC at $k{=}16$ falls to 0.368 and is below 0.5 in most draws at $k{=}8$ and $k{=}16$, (6 of 10 and 8 of 10 draws), while $k{=}64$ recovers to 0.942, still 0.023 below FLARE-2.
We report this as observed and do not have a verified explanation for the inverted ranking.
\emph{Validation.} Selecting the checkpoint on in-source validation episodes instead of a held-out source changes binary AUC by at most 0.009 on both targets, so we cannot show that held-out validation matters on these two targets.
\emph{Loss mask.} Masking queries without support gives similar binary AUC on CTU-13 and higher AUC at $k{\ge}16$ on CIC-IDS2017 (0.890 against 0.861 at $k{=}16$), with higher $N$-way macro-F1 on both targets.
\emph{Class-level tasks.} Training on binary tasks only lowers binary AUC on CTU-13 by 0.01 to 0.04 and on CIC-IDS2017 by 0.02 to 0.03 at $k{\ge}16$, while the per-class AUC at $k{=}4$ is nearly unchanged (0.858 against 0.863 and 0.959 against 0.963) and $N$-way macro-F1 at $k{=}16$ is 0.566 against 0.582 on CIC-IDS2017.
Class-level tasks help the binary score modestly, and we find no evidence that they are the main source of the class-level results.
